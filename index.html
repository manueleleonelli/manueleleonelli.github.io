I am an Associate Professor of Statistics in the School of Science and Technology at IE University. My research develops interpretable statistical and machine learning models, mostly from a Bayesian perspective, and studies their mathematical properties. My main areas of research are:

  • Probabilistic graphical models
  • Sensitivity analysis
  • Extreme value theory
  • Causal inference and causal discovery
  • Interpretable AI and its role in society
  • Applications in finance, energy markets, the environment, sport and tourism
In particular, I have been focusing on a class of models called staged trees. Together with Gherardo Varando I developed the stagedtrees R package, available on CRAN, and we have written the book Staged Tree Models: With Applications in R, forthcoming with CRC Press.
